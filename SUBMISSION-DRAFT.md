## Title

DossierMind — Evidence before advice

## Short description

An AMD-powered document workspace connecting clients and accountants through a clickable brain map, source-linked AI proposals and draft reports. Built with synthetic data and explicit review boundaries.

## Long description

DossierMind helps a client and an accountant understand a shared document archive
without losing the distinction between original evidence and AI interpretation.
Its competition demo uses two entirely fabricated client archives and three
protected demonstration profiles.

Original documents remain encrypted and separately authorized by client. PDF,
spreadsheet, CSV, XML and email extraction creates source-linked passages. A
clickable brain-shaped map connects documents, wiki proposals and attributed
contributions, letting a reviewer return to the underlying source.

Server-side AMD inference powers document wiki proposals, scanned-page
transcription and an Advisor that separates quotations from interpretations and
proposed actions. Quotations are checked against supplied passages. Visual output
can be compared with its original page. When evidence is insufficient, the system
can abstain rather than fabricate an answer. Draft PDF reports preserve provenance
and review limitations.

This is a working competition prototype, not certified accounting advice or a
commercially finished service. Retrieval is bounded and lexical; exact quotation
checks do not establish semantic correctness, payment, tax deductibility or complete
coverage. AI results require professional review. The public release contains only
demo code and fabricated examples; private business materials, customer archives,
credentials and runtime databases are excluded.

Preexisting components are disclosed in PROVENANCE.json. AMD integration and
subsequent prototype increments are documented through actual repository commits.
