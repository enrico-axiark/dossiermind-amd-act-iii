- [Pitch PDF](dossiermind-pitch-final.pdf): ten slides describing the intake,
  shared document brain, AMD inference and the position before accounting software.
- [Cover image](dossiermind-cover-final.png): 1920 x 1080, 16:9 PNG.
- [Demo video](dossiermind-demo-final.mp4): approximately 2 minutes 56 seconds,

  recording or a performance measurement.
- [Reviewer guide](dossiermind-reviewer-guide.pdf): public walkthrough and scope.

The video follows mixed-document intake, INGESTIONLAB preparation, sourced AMD
wiki proposals, scanned-page comparison, the clickable brain, a factual Advisor
answer, accountant access and draft PDF output. Parsing and model requests have
separate actions and states. Advice requests can return insufficient evidence;
the demonstration does not promise verified recommendations or economic gains.

All displayed people, companies and documents are fabricated. No account
passwords, API keys, private executive documents, commercial contacts or master
materials are included. Reviewer passwords require a separate private channel.

Product positioning adapts the project's preexisting executive message. Component
reuse remains disclosed in `../PROVENANCE.json`; these materials do not resolve
organizer eligibility review. The application is a competition prototype.
